over the generic brief. This does not support adding it to our verification protocol as a fix for dropped qualifiers."

## Reading
- A procedure telling verifiers to list each claim's source restrictions did not find more deleted qualifiers. It did roughly
  triple false findings on the planted copies.
- On these fresh plants the generic brief caught 44% of deleted qualifiers, not the 0% of wave 2. The blind spot is partial, not
  total. Possible causes: this set was harder to hide, verifiers read the report's own "0 of 12" sentence (D1 priming), and 6 of 9
  plants had their qualifier stated elsewhere in the package.
- Adjudication: κ = 1.0 on the 46 plant-locating findings and κ = 0.69 overall; 46 disagreements were resolved by a third
  adjudicator (all PREEXISTING).
- Deviations: D1 (priming), D2 (Q09 dropped), D3 (brief delivered as a file), D4 (the brief-copy step failed; 8 runs were
  restarted before reading any package content), D5 (9 runs listed sibling folder names while the brief was missing; kept).
- Scope: one procedure, one document set, 9 distinct plants, 2 runs per copy per arm, same-model verifiers and adjudicators.
  This is not a general claim.
