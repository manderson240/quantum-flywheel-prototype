# Pre-registration — SemIf 0.8B as a claim-entailment check on our own report (frozen 2026-09-24, before any scoring)

Question: can SemIf/OpenJev 0.8B NLI (MIT; measured AUROC 0.977 on a different task, 2026-09-22) tell a report sentence that
the evidence supports from one it does not, in this project's physics prose?

Items: 10 pairs. Each pair = one premise (evidence text, generated by code from results/*.json or quoted verbatim from a
pre-registration file) + two hypotheses: a SUPPORTED sentence and an UNSUPPORTED one. Unsupported sentences are the pre-fix
report sentences the independent verifier flagged (reviews/verifier_independent_20260924.md) or my earlier wrong statements
from this session (e.g. "72/72", "0.19-0.39"); supported ones are the corrected versions. The author wrote both the items and
the labels — this is a pilot, not a validation.

Score: p = P(entailment). Metrics: (1) pairwise accuracy = pairs where p(supported) > p(unsupported);
(2) AUROC over the 20 sentences; (3) control: AUROC of hypothesis length alone; (4) sanity control that must pass first:
"Paris is the capital of France." entailed by "France's capital city is Paris." above "Berlin is the capital of France.".

ADOPT as a drafting aid (never as evidence in the application) only if: sanity control passes AND pairwise >= 9/10 AND
AUROC >= length-control AUROC + 0.10. Otherwise DROP. No re-wording of items or hypotheses after scoring.
Memory: runs only when MemAvailable >= 24 GiB; single CPU process ~2 GB, nice 15.
